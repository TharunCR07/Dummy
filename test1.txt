BrokerOnboard - Retail Brokerage Account Opening 
& KYC Orchestration System
1. Introduction
BrokerOnboard is a web-based Retail Brokerage Account Opening & KYC Orchestration
platform for broker-dealers and investment platforms to streamline prospect onboarding, 
e-KYC/KYB, risk profiling/suitability, product consents, agreements/e-sign, in-person 
verification (ref), nominee & FATCA/CRS, and account approval with trading segment 
activation (e.g., equity/cash, F&O, currency, commodities; margin facilities).
It provides maker–checker workflows, configurable rules, audit-ready trails, and integrates 
with downstream back-office / depository / exchange gateways (Phase-1 as internal 
references).
Architecture: REST API-based backend with Angular/React frontend; compatible with Java 
(Spring Boot) or .NET (ASP.NET Core).
Actors / Users
• Investor / Applicant: Initiates application, submits KYC docs, completes 
declarations and e-sign.
• Sales/Partner Agent: Assisted onboarding, referral tracking, status follow-ups.
• KYC Verifier / Ops Maker: Verifies documents, video IPV (ref), addresses 
pendencies.
• Compliance Checker: Reviews AML/PEP red flags, approves/rejects applications.
• Account Admin: Activates trading segments, depository IDs, agreements & 
consents.
• System Admin: Configures workflows, rules, document templates, and reports.

7. User Interface Design
• Applicant Portal: Stepper flow (profile → KYC → bank link → FATCA/CRS → risk 
profile → e-sign → submit), pendency tracker.
• Agent Console: Prospects, assisted applications, funnel status, follow-ups.
• Ops/KYC Workbench: Document verification queue, IPV/video references, 
address/bank checks.
• Compliance Console: AML results, escalations, decisions, audit trails.
• Approvals Desk: Maker–checker tasks, segment activation, finalize approvals.
• Admin Panel: Rules, forms, agreements, e-sign templates, users/roles.
• Reports Dashboard: Funnel, TAT, drop-offs, approval/rejection ratios by channel.
8. Non-Functional Requirements
• Performance: Support 50,000 concurrent users during campaign spikes; fast step 
transitions.
• Security: PII protection, encryption (at rest/in transit), RBAC, immutable audit, 
consent capture.
• Scalability: Horizontal scaling for queues (KYC verify/AML checks/e-sign 
reminders).
• Availability: 99.9% uptime; graceful degradation for analytics/report batches.
• Maintainability: Modular microservices; config-driven 
questionnaires/rules/agreements.
• Observability: Centralized logs; KPIs (funnel progression, TAT, drop-off rates, 
approval ratios); alerting.
9. Assumptions & Constraints
• Phase-1 excludes live integrations to KRA/KYC registries, AML bureaus, bank 
account verification gateways, depository/exchanges—stored as references.
• e-sign is reference only (captures status and external reference).
• No payment gateway or fund settlement in Phase-1.
• Notifications are in-app only.
• Fully implementable using Java Spring Boot/ASP.NET Core + Angular/React with
